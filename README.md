### What I do

- 🏙️ **Now:** Technical Lead at **DKSR**, leading engineering on a cloud-native Urban Data Platform

- 📈 **Before:** 11 years at **Fidelity Investments** as Senior Engineer, Tech Lead and Principal
  Engineer on fixed-income e-trading: real-money, multi-region systems where errors are not an option.
- 🧠 **Also:** shipping production RAG features with LangChain4j and pgvector, and bringing
  AI-assisted development into everyday team workflows.
- 🧑‍🏫 I enjoy mentoring, code review and turning messy architectures into ones teams can reason about.
